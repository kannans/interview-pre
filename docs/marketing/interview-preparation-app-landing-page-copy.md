## Features Section

### Section Heading

Everything you need to prepare better

### Feature 1: Personalized Preparation Roadmap

Choose your experience level, focus areas, and target companies. Get a practical plan that tells you what to study first, what to practice next, and where your biggest gaps are.

### Feature 2: Basic Programming Recovery

If you are struggling with basic coding questions, the app helps you rebuild confidence from scratch with beginner-to-intermediate practice in logic, loops, functions, strings, arrays, and clean coding.

### Feature 3: DSA Preparation

Practice the most common interview topics including arrays, strings, linked lists, trees, graphs, recursion, dynamic programming, sliding window, and more with guided explanations and coding exercises.

### Feature 4: Frontend and React Preparation

Prepare for JavaScript, React, hooks, performance, debugging, browser concepts, and machine coding rounds with targeted frontend interview support.

### Feature 5: Mock Interviews with AI

Practice with an AI interviewer that asks questions, follows up, evaluates your answers, and helps you improve across coding, DSA, frontend, behavioral, and system design rounds.

### Feature 6: CV and Job Description Fit Analysis

Upload your CV, compare it with a job description, and understand how well your profile matches the role, what is missing, and what to improve before applying.

### Feature 7: Company-Specific Preparation

Select your target companies and get guidance on likely interview rounds, question patterns, focus topics, and preparation strategy.

### Feature 8: Readiness Scoring and Feedback

Know where you stand after every session with performance feedback, improvement suggestions, and a realistic readiness indicator.
