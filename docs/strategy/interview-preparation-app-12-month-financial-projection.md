# Interview Preparation Coach 12-Month Financial Projection

## Document Purpose

This document provides a simple 12-month startup projection for the focused version of the product:

`AI interview recovery coach for experienced engineers`

It is designed to help with:

- founder planning
- pitch deck finance assumptions
- early burn estimation
- break-even thinking

## Key Assumptions

These are simplified planning assumptions, not final business forecasts.

### Product and Pricing Assumptions

- average monthly revenue per paying user: `1,800 INR`
- sprint pack price: `4,999 INR`
- expert mock add-on: `2,999 INR`

### User Growth Assumptions

- total users grow gradually month by month
- monthly active users are around `20%` of total users
- paying users start low and grow steadily
- upsell purchases begin small and increase with traction

### Cost Assumptions

- base fixed operating cost starts lean and rises slowly
- AI usage scales with active users
- marketing increases as the product gains traction
- maintenance and tooling expand over time

## Projection Logic

### Revenue

`Subscription Revenue = Paying Users x Average Revenue Per Paying User`

`Sprint Revenue = Sprint Pack Sales x Sprint Pack Price`

`Mock Revenue = Expert Mock Sales x Mock Session Price`

`Total Revenue = Subscription Revenue + Sprint Revenue + Mock Revenue`

### Cost

`Total OPEX = Fixed Operating Cost + AI Usage Cost + Marketing Cost`

### Profit / Loss

`Profit / Loss = Total Revenue - Total OPEX`

## What This Projection Suggests

This model suggests:

- the product may run at a loss for the first `4 months`
- break-even may begin around `Month 5`
- premium upsells make a meaningful difference
- subscription alone is helpful, but the business becomes much healthier when sprint packs and mock add-ons grow

## Simple Revenue Breakdown Example

### Month 12 Example

- paying users: `260`
- average revenue per paying user: `1,800 INR`
- subscription revenue: `468,000 INR`
- sprint pack sales: `28 x 4,999 = 139,972 INR`
- expert mock sales: `15 x 2,999 = 44,985 INR`

## OPEX Interpretation

The OPEX growth in this model includes:

- hosting and infra growth
- AI cost scaling with usage
- marketing investment as demand grows
- maintenance and support increase

It does not include:

- large full-time team expansion
- office costs
- enterprise sales motion
- heavy legal or compliance overhead

So this remains a lean founder-stage model.

## Break-Even Insight

In this model, break-even is achieved because:

- paying users grow steadily
- upsells are introduced early
- OPEX remains controlled

If any of these fail, break-even moves later.

## Risk Sensitivity

The projection can weaken quickly if:

- paid conversion is lower than expected
- AI cost per active user rises sharply
- users do not buy sprint packs
- retention is weak
- customer acquisition becomes expensive
