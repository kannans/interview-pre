# Interview Preparation Coach Pitch Deck Financial Slides

## Document Purpose

This document converts the financial planning work into pitch-deck-ready slide content for the focused product:

`AI interview recovery coach for experienced engineers`

The goal is to help present:

- business model
- pricing
- cost structure
- revenue logic
- break-even direction

in a simple and credible way.

## Important Pitch Guidance

For an early-stage pitch deck, investors usually do not expect perfect financial precision.

They do expect:

- clear business model
- believable assumptions
- realistic cost awareness
- thoughtful path to monetization
- evidence that unit economics can work

## Recommended Financial Slide Sequence

Use the following finance-related slides in order:

1. Business Model
2. Pricing Strategy
3. Revenue Streams
4. Cost Structure
5. Unit Economics
6. 12-Month Projection
7. Break-Even Outlook

## Slide 1: Business Model

### Slide Title

Business Model

### Slide Content

- Freemium entry point for interview-readiness assessment
- Monthly subscription for roadmap, AI mock interviews, and recovery tracks
- Premium upsells for sprint plans and expert mock sessions
- Long-term opportunity for B2B and cohort licensing

### Presenter Notes

Our business model is intentionally simple. We use a free-to-paid conversion path for serious candidates, then expand revenue with high-intent premium offers tied to interview outcomes.

## Slide 2: Pricing Strategy

### Slide Title

Pricing Strategy

### Slide Content

| Offer | Sample Price |
|---|---:|
| Free Plan | 0 INR |
| Pro Monthly | 1,499 INR/month |
| Premium Monthly | 3,499 INR/month |
| Sprint Pack | 4,999 INR one-time |
| Expert Mock Add-On | 2,999 INR/session |

### Presenter Notes

The pricing is designed around career-outcome value rather than content volume. Users preparing for higher-paying roles are more willing to pay for structured recovery and targeted mock practice.

## Slide 3: Revenue Streams

### Slide Title

Revenue Streams

### Slide Content

- Recurring subscription revenue
- Interview sprint pack revenue
- Expert mock interview add-on revenue
- Future B2B licensing revenue

### Presenter Notes

Subscriptions create recurring revenue, but premium outcome-focused products improve margins and reduce dependence on subscription-only economics.

## Slide 4: Cost Structure

### Slide Title

Cost Structure

### Slide Content

Main monthly cost drivers:

- AI usage cost
- cloud hosting and database
- storage and monitoring tools
- engineering maintenance
- marketing and customer acquisition

Main one-time startup costs:

- MVP build
- design and UX
- brand and setup
- legal and launch preparation

### Presenter Notes

This product is capital-light compared with traditional businesses, but it is sensitive to AI usage cost and retention quality. Cost control matters as much as growth.

## Slide 5: Unit Economics

### Slide Title

Unit Economics

### Slide Content

Sample assumptions:

- Average monthly revenue per paying user: `1,800 INR`
- Estimated direct variable cost per paying user: `300 INR`
- Estimated gross profit per paying user: `1,500 INR`

Key drivers:

- paid conversion rate
- retention
- AI cost per active user
- upsell take rate

### Presenter Notes

The business becomes attractive when AI cost per user stays under control and premium users convert into higher-margin products like sprint packs and expert sessions.

## Slide 6: 12-Month Projection

### Slide Title

12-Month Financial Outlook

### Slide Content

Sample Year 1 outlook:

- Total Year 1 Revenue: `3.04M INR`
- Total Year 1 OPEX: `2.35M INR`
- Estimated Year 1 Operating Result: `689K INR`

Sample Month 12 snapshot:

- Total users: `4,500`
- Active users: `900`
- Paying users: `260`
- Monthly revenue: `648,957 INR`
- Monthly OPEX: `410,000 INR`
- Monthly operating result: `238,957 INR`

### Presenter Notes

This is a founder planning model, not a guaranteed forecast. It shows that the business can work if we maintain disciplined spending, healthy conversion, and early premium upsells.

## Slide 7: Break-Even Outlook

### Slide Title

Break-Even Outlook

### Slide Content

Sample break-even logic:

- Monthly OPEX target: `255,000 INR`
- Gross profit per paying user: `1,500 INR`
- Estimated break-even paying users: `170`

Projected break-even timing in the sample model:

- around `Month 5`

### Presenter Notes

The exact month will shift based on conversion, AI usage cost, and marketing efficiency. The important point is that the path to break-even is visible and modelable.

## Suggested Visuals for Deck

Use simple visuals such as:

## What Not to Do in the Deck

- do not overload slides with too many assumptions
- do not show unrealistic hockey-stick growth without explanation
- do not hide AI cost sensitivity
- do not present speculative revenue as certainty

## Best Founder Narrative

The best finance narrative is:

- this is a focused niche with painful demand
- the business has recurring revenue potential
- premium offers strengthen economics
- AI cost is manageable with good product design
- the path to break-even is visible early

## Short Deck Summary

The financial section of the deck should show that:

- users have a strong reason to pay
- the business is not purely dependent on one revenue source
- costs are understood
- growth can lead to sustainable economics
